About Me

Hi, I'm Edward Anthony Masen Cullen . I was born in Chicago in 1901 and transformed into a vampire in 1918 after nearly dying from the Spanish flu.

I live in Forks, Washington with my adoptive family, the Cullens. Even though I'm over 100 years old, I remain physically 17 forever.

My special ability is reading minds — except for Bella Swan's. She's the love of my immortal life.
